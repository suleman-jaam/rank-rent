Moisture Patterns Across the Spokane Valley Basin

Spokane Valley's geography is defined by the expansive valley floor and the underlying Spokane Valley-Rathdrum Prairie Aquifer. Because the aquifer runs relatively close to surface grades in specific low-lying sectors, prolonged spring snowmelt and heavy lawn irrigation often result in sub-grade moisture pressure against basement slabs and crawl spaces.

Many of the established mid-century residential neighborhoods feature uninsulated crawl space plumbing and original single-pane windows where cold-weather condensation triggers sill mold. Our technicians understand these conditions and pair physical mold removal with structural moisture control.
