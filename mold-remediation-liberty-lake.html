Managing Lakefront Humidity & Modern Home Envelopes

Liberty Lake combines natural lakeside topography with modern energy-efficient home construction. While tight thermal envelopes conserve heating in winter, they can also trap indoor moisture produced by daily living if ventilation systems are improperly balanced.

Homes near the lakefront and along hillside drainages frequently encounter groundwater runoff, unsealed crawl space soils, and high basement relative humidity during summer months. Our certified team identifies hidden moisture pockets behind finished drywall and installs heavy-duty encapsulation systems to keep homes dry.
