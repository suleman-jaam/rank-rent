Addressing Plateau Wind Exposure & Moisture Intrusion

Airway Heights sits elevated on the West Plains basalt plateau, where winter storms bring sustained winds that push moisture into unshielded building envelopes, soffits, and exterior plumbing chases. Many newer single-family developments feature unconditioned crawl spaces that collect seasonal dampness from shallow soil layers.

Additionally, slab-on-grade homes can trap condensation underneath vinyl plank flooring when indoor humidity rises. Our technicians utilize non-destructive moisture meters and thermal imaging to locate dampness behind walls and under floors, removing mold and ensuring permanent moisture stability.
