Diagnostic indicators

Common Problems Solved by Mold Testing

Don't guess with your family's health or property investment. Look for these warning signs:

ISSUE

Persistent musty or earthy odors

Even when mold is concealed behind baseboards or inside ceilings, microbial volatile organic compounds (mVOCs) produce telltale odors that our testing verifies.

SYMPTOM

Unexplained allergy or sinus reactions

Itching eyes, morning congestion, coughing, and headaches that ease when leaving the house indicate elevated airborne mold spore loads.

SIGN

Mystery wall stains or peeling paint

Bubbling latex paint, yellow-brown water halos, or fuzzy black, white, or greenish patches demand immediate moisture mapping and laboratory swab testing.

What is the difference between mold inspection and mold testing in Spokane?

A mold inspection is a thorough visual and physical examination of the building structure using thermal imaging, moisture meters, and visual diagnostics to locate moisture sources. Mold testing collects physical air spore traps, surface tape lifts, or swab samples that are analyzed by an independent accredited laboratory to identify specific mold species (e.g., Stachybotrys, Aspergillus) and spore concentration levels.

How much does mold inspection and testing cost in Spokane WA?

In Spokane, professional mold inspections typically range from $150 to $350 for a standard visual and moisture assessment. When certified laboratory air and surface samples are required, complete testing packages generally range from $300 to $650, depending on the square footage and number of sampling zones needed.

When should Spokane homeowners schedule a mold inspection?

You should schedule a professional mold inspection if you detect a persistent musty smell, observe unexplained staining or discoloration on drywall or ceilings, have suffered recent water leaks or roof snowmelt intrusion, are buying or selling a home in Spokane, or family members experience unexplainable allergic or respiratory symptoms.

How fast do lab testing results come back?

Our laboratory air and surface samples are delivered to an accredited independent microbiology lab. Standard results are typically ready in 24 to 48 hours, with rush expedited processing available for real estate closings and emergency situations.
